How it works

  1. 1

    Take or pick the photo

    The camera shows the format's frame and the crown, eye and chin guides. You can also pick a photo from your library.

  2. 2

    Frame it

    Adjust with your fingers under the same guides. If you like, “White background” separates the person from the background, on the iPhone itself, and “Adjust” corrects brightness, warmth and tint.

  3. 3

    Print

    One 4×6 in sheet with cut marks and nothing else: no logos or watermarks. At home as a PDF, or at a shop or photo lab as a JPEG.

The app

Formats

35×45 mm

Passports and ID cards in most EU countries. 8 copies per sheet.

26×32 mm

Spanish DNI and passport, NIE and TIE. 12 copies per sheet.

Printing

At home (PDF)

Tap Print at home (PDF), then Print. 4×6 in (10×15) paper at 100% scale, not “fit”. Measure a copy with a ruler.

At a shop or photo lab (JPEG)

Tap Shop or photo lab (JPEG) and save or send the image. Order 4×6 in prints without cropping or fitting: it is 1200×1800 pixels at 300 dpi, exactly 4×6 in.

Odd colours? If the photo or the print comes out purple, yellowish or bluish, tap Adjust on the crop screen: Tint towards green removes purple, and Warmth corrects cold or warm light. A white background stays white. Before printing, tap the sheet to see it full screen.

Will my photo be accepted?

The photos are the official size, but each office decides whether to accept them, and some refuse retouched photos, such as those with a white background. The DNI guides are approximate: what is required is a plain white background, the whole face from the front, and open eyes.

Privacy

Everything happens on your iPhone: no photo leaves the phone, there is no account and no analytics. Privacy policy.
