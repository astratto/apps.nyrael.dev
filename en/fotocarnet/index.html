ID Photos: EU & DNI

ID photos at the official size, from a single photo, on your iPhone. Free, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

  • Formats: the European passport and document format (35×45 mm) and the Spanish DNI (26×32 mm).
  • A camera with guides for the crown, eyes and chin, or a photo from your library.
  • An optional white background, computed on the iPhone itself.
  • One 4×6 in sheet with 8 EU or 12 DNI copies, with cut marks.

Printing at home

On the sheet, tap Print at home (PDF), then Print. Choose 4×6 in (10×15) paper and 100% scale, not “fit”. Measure a copy with a ruler: it should be 35×45 or 26×32 mm.

Printing at a shop or photo lab

Tap Shop or photo lab (JPEG) and save or send the image. Order 4×6 in prints without cropping or fitting. The image is 1200×1800 pixels at 300 dpi, exactly 4×6 in.

Will my photo be accepted?

The photos are the official size, but each office decides whether to accept them, and some refuse retouched photos, such as those with a white background. The DNI guides are approximate: what is required is a plain white background, the whole face from the front, and open eyes.

Privacy

Everything happens on your iPhone; no photo ever leaves the phone. Privacy policy.
