Privacy policy

ID Photos: EU & DNI collects no data at all.

  • The app has no account, ads, analytics or tracking, and does not connect to the internet.
  • The photos you take or choose are processed only on your iPhone, including the white background, and are sent nowhere.
  • The PDF and JPEG files are made in the app's temporary folder and only leave the iPhone if you share them. iOS clears that folder when it needs to.
  • The app asks for the camera only to take the photo. Choosing a photo from your library does not give it access to the whole library.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first. Questions: apps@nyrael.dev.
