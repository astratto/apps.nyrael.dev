What's new

What each version brings. Your version is in the app, under Help › About the app.

1.1.0 Waiting for Apple

It reaches your iPhone once Apple approves it.

  • Automatic framing: the app finds the face, eyes, chin and top of the hair, and places the photo on the guides. "Frame" puts it back after you move it.
  • The camera helps you aim: the guides turn gold and the phone vibrates when the face is in place, and a hint says what to change ("Move closer", "Lower the phone"…).
  • Hair slider: keep more long hair, or cut tighter, with the white background.
  • Digital photo: a single photo at 600 dpi for online forms.
  • A warning that paper other than 4×6 in changes the size of the photos.

1.0.1 On the App Store

17 October 2026. The first version.

  • A 4×6 in sheet with 8 EU (35×45) or 12 Spanish DNI (26×32) photos, with cut marks and no watermark.
  • A camera with guides for the crown, eyes and chin, and a 3 s timer; or a photo from your library.
  • White background, on the iPhone itself, and Adjust: brightness, warmth and tint.
  • At home as a PDF, or at a shop or photo lab as a JPEG; the sheet full screen to check it.
